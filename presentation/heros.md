Seven heroes are ready to play. Bring up to four of them into the dungeon together (two of the same still works, if you insist):

- **Ranger**: quick and precise, he peppers everything with the **Repeater Bow**. His power, **Volley**, fires three rapid bursts of five bolts.
- **Bastion**: solid as a wall, **Scatter Crossbow** in hand. His **Shockwave** knocks back everything that gets too close.
- **Duelist**: sword in hand and a grin on his face. His **Whirlwind** spins three times, blade out, and every melee kill brings his dodge back sooner.
- **Wizard**: his **Apprentice Wand** shoots sparks and **Frost Wave** chills a whole cone in front of him. His power takes 20% less time to recharge. With only 85 health, he'll need it.
- **Sledge** has worn out three anvils. With 135 health and a two-handed quarry hammer, he can afford to stand his ground. His melee hits deal 20% more damage and take longer between swings. **Quarry Blow** knocks back enemies in a wide arc in front of him.
- **Bruiser**: fists first, questions later. She jabs fast with the **Forge Gauntlets**, her **Fist Flurry** throws six quick punches in front of her, and every melee kill gives her a short shield.
- **Sparkler**: her **Spark Caster** shoots sparks that jump from one enemy to the next, and her **Discharge** sets off a close burst that leaps through the crowd.

The last two come from **The Sunken Forge**. Everyone has a **dodge** to slip through blows, and a **power** that recharges on its own.

Made for fingers on a tablet: **Volte**, the gliding duelist. Hold the dodge and she glides for up to a second, steered by your thumb around a foe; let go when the gold lights up behind its back, and she strikes it hard and gets her glide straight back. She comes as a pack: switch her on in **Options**, then **Packs**. Switch on **The Frostbitten Crypt Trapper** as well for the pickaxe-wielding **Trapper**. With both optional heroes, the full roster is nine.

![Five of the starting heroes, each casting their power, with their weapon and power below](presentation/images/heros.webp)

A weapon found once stays in your **Arsenal**: next run, you can start with it. The Arsenal also keeps track of your powers and armor.

In the pause, **Character** shows each hero of the run on one screen: life, speed and dodge, armor, the weapon and the power with their damage as it stands, and every card picked so far, with what it does.
