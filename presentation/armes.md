Thirty-one weapons, two ways to hit. Here are the original fifteen. From afar with the **Repeater Bow**, the **Scatter Crossbow**, the **Rivet Crossbow**, the **Spark Caster** and the **Blowpipe**. Up close with the **Sword**, the **Axe**, the **Spear**, the **Mace**, the **Forge Gauntlets**, the **Glaive**, the **Forge Hammer**, the **Twin Daggers**, the **Scythe** and the **Flail**.

Each has its own rhythm. The sword chains three blows, the axe sweeps wide, the spear reaches far, and the mace is slow but stuns what it hits and smashes right through a guard. The gauntlets jab fast and finish with a big one, the glaive slashes wide from far off, and the hammer is heavy: every blow that lands sends out a shockwave. The daggers cut four times in a breath, the last one twice as hard; the scythe reaps everything in front and to the sides; the flail swings a full circle around you. At range, a rivet goes through two more enemies, a spark jumps to two more nearby, and the blowpipe spits darts that bounce off once.

Then there's the **Cast-Iron Skillet**, the **Gust Bellows** and the **Pinball Trombone**. Aboard the ship at Rock Bottom, look for the **Sun-Cutter Beam**, the **Prism Gauntlet** and the **Hull-Welder Torch**.

The spear and the mace only turn up from the third fight on. The forge's weapons, including the glaive, Rivet Crossbow and Forge Hammer, start dropping in the second stratum. The four weapons of **Toadstool Hollow** only turn up from the third stratum. The forge's and the Hollow's weapons drop in every world.

Weapons drop from crates, chests, elites and the big monsters, in six rarities:

- **Common**, in gray: the basics.
- **Uncommon**, in green: a little more damage.
- **Rare**, in blue: more still, plus an effect such as **of Embers** (sets what it hits ablaze), **of Frost** (slows it down), **of the Anvil** (direct hits set off a small blast), **of Lightning** (a bolt jumps to two more enemies) or **of the Leech** (kills heal you). It lands under a column of light you can see from across the room.
- **Epic**, in purple: more damage again, an effect and a weapon trait.
- **Legendary**, in gold: two effects on the same weapon.
- **Mythic**, in rose red: two effects, a weapon trait and the hardest hit of the six.

The **Toad King** gives an Epic weapon to every hero; the first **Big Bolete** of a stratum, an Epic each; and the **Golden Chest** of the Hollow, one more.

Walk up to a weapon: a label compares its damage, speed and reach with what you're holding. If it's better, take it. Your old weapon stays on the floor, for a friend.

Armor can change how you fight, too. A **Runic Mantle** brings your power back sooner. A **Tortoise Cuirass** makes you slower, but when a monster shoves you, you stand your ground.

For **the Unique**, bring a weapon you're willing to part with. The gates of strata **5, 10 and 15** each give you a piece of a scroll. With the third piece in hand, lay a weapon down at the gate. It keeps its properties and takes on magic from your allies, or from the scroll if you're alone. The finished weapon lands on the floor for someone to claim. You get one per run.
